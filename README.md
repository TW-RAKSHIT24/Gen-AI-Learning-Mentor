

The **GenAI Learning Mentor** is an intelligent educational platform designed to provide personalized learning support to students. It uses Generative AI, Prompt Engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and AI Agent technology to create an interactive learning experience. Students can upload course notes and learning resources, ask questions, generate quizzes, and receive explanations based on their study materials. The system analyzes quiz results to identify weak and strong areas and automatically creates personalized study plans according to the student's performance, learning level, available study time, and exam schedule. The adaptive tutoring feature adjusts the difficulty of explanations and practice questions based on the student's progress. An AI Learning Coach further guides students by recommending what to study next and generating targeted practice questions. Overall, the project acts as a virtual learning companion that helps students understand concepts, improve weak areas, practice effectively, and prepare better for examinations.
